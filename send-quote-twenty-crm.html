How to send a quote from Twenty and get it accepted

Send a quote from a Twenty deal, let your client accept it online, and have the deal update itself.

Updated 13 November 2026

This guide uses QuoteFlowin, the quotation app for Twenty CRM. If you haven't built a quote yet, start with how to create quotes in Twenty CRM.

1. Send the quote

  1. On the deal's Quote tab, click Send quote and confirm.
  2. The quote gets its number (for example Q-0003), a private link, and a ready-written email.
  3. Click Open in email app to send it from your own mailbox, or Copy email to paste it anywhere. Replies come straight to you.

The quote is locked while it's out, so what your client sees can't change under them.

2. What your client sees

  • A clean page with your logo and brand color. No account or login needed.
  • Line items, discount, tax and total, the valid-until date, your message, terms and payment instructions.
  • An Accept quote button: they type their name to accept. Or they can decline and say why.
  • A page that prints to a tidy PDF for their records.

Links use a long random secret and are hidden from search engines.

3. Know when it's opened

The Quote tab shows when your client first opened the link. Your own clicks don't count. Sent, viewed, accepted and declined also appear on the deal's timeline, and the By status board shows every quote with totals per column.

4. When they accept

  • The quote is marked Accepted with the client's name.
  • The deal moves to your won stage (set in the app's settings; the default is CUSTOMER).
  • The deal amount is set to the quote total.
  • Any other open quotes on the same deal are marked superseded, so only one can be accepted.

5. When they decline

You see the reason they gave. Click Revise and resend to adjust the quote and send a new version.

Changing a sent quote

Withdraw it first. The old link stops working right away, so your client can't accept outdated prices. Edit the quote, then send it again.

Get faster yeses

  • Keep the message short: what the quote covers and what happens after they accept.
  • Set a validity date. A clear deadline helps clients decide.
  • Add payment instructions so they know how to pay once they accept.
  • Follow up once it's been opened. The Quote tab tells you when.

QuoteFlowin doesn't do e-signatures or online payment of quotes yet. Acceptance is recorded with the client's typed name.
