FAQ

Questions about pricing

What does "per workspace" mean?

You pay once for your CRM workspace. Everyone in it can create and send quotes. No per-seat fees.

What's included in the free trial?

14 days and up to 10 quotes, with line items, public quote links and client acceptance. No credit card required.

Do paid plans have a quote limit?

No. Monthly, Annual and Lifetime all include unlimited quotes. Only the free trial is capped at 10.

How much do I save with Annual?

Annual is $9 per workspace per month, billed $108 a year. That's 25% less than paying $12 monthly.

What's the Early Bird Lifetime deal?

One payment of $139 for unlimited quotes, forever, with no recurring fees. It's limited to the first 75 workspaces and will be removed once claimed.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly can be cancelled at any time.

Which CRMs does it work with?

Twenty CRM today, on Twenty Cloud and self-hosted. Attio and other CRMs are coming soon. A self-hosted Twenty server must be reachable from the internet so clients can open quote links.

Where is my quote data stored?

In your own Twenty workspace, next to your deals. It's covered by your workspace's backups and permissions.

Something else?

Email info@quoteflowin.com. A real person will answer.
