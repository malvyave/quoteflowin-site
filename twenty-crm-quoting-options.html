Quoting options for Twenty CRM, compared

Three ways to send quotes from Twenty, and which fits your team.

Updated 13 November 2026 · Written by the QuoteFlowin team, so weigh it accordingly.

Summary

  • A quoting app inside Twenty, such as QuoteFlowin: fastest setup, quotes live on the deal, and acceptance updates the deal.
  • Build it yourself with Twenty's custom objects and workflows: the most flexible, and the most work.
  • A separate quoting or proposal tool: richer documents, e-signatures and payments, but your quotes live outside Twenty.

A quoting app inside Twenty

QuoteFlowin adds a Quote tab to every deal. You add line items with discount, tax and currency, brand the quote with your logo and color, and send a private link. The client accepts or declines online, and on acceptance the deal moves to your won stage with the quote total as its amount. Quote data stays in your Twenty workspace.

Good for: teams who want quotes tied to their pipeline without building anything. Not yet: per-line tax, e-signatures, online payment.

Build it yourself in Twenty

Twenty lets you add custom objects, such as Quote and Line item, and automate them with workflows. To quote clients this way, you also need to work out totals, discount and tax, quote numbering, a page the client can open without a Twenty login, a way to record acceptance, and the update to the deal.

Good for: teams with developer time and unusual quoting rules.

A separate quoting or proposal tool

Proposal tools such as PandaDoc, Proposify or Qwilr make rich, designed documents, and often include e-signatures and payments. The catch is that quotes live outside your CRM. Check whether the tool connects to Twenty; otherwise someone copies amounts and stages back by hand.

Good for: long, designed proposals, or when you need legally binding e-signatures today.

Which to choose

  • You want quotes on your deals this week, at a flat price: QuoteFlowin.
  • You have unusual pricing rules and a developer: build it yourself.
  • You need e-signatures or online payment right now: a separate tool.
