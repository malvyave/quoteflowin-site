How to create quotes in Twenty CRM

Build an itemized quote on a Twenty deal, brand it, and get it ready to send to your client.

Updated 13 November 2026

The short answer

Install QuoteFlowin, the quotation app for Twenty CRM. It adds a Quote tab to every deal (opportunity). Add your line items, check the live preview, and send the client a private link. When they accept, the deal is marked won with the quote total as its amount.

Your options

There are three ways to quote from Twenty:

  1. A quoting app inside Twenty, like QuoteFlowin. Quotes live on the deal, and acceptance updates the deal for you.
  2. Build it yourself with Twenty's custom objects and workflows. Flexible, but you also need totals, numbering, a page your client can open, and a way to record acceptance.
  3. A separate quoting or proposal tool, then copy the amount back into Twenty by hand or through an integration.

See quoting options for Twenty CRM, compared for the trade-offs. The rest of this guide uses QuoteFlowin.

Step by step

  1. Install QuoteFlowin. Open the install page while signed in to Twenty and click Authorize. The free trial starts right away: 14 days or 10 quotes, no card needed.
  2. Add your company details. In the app's settings in Twenty, fill in your seller name, logo URL, address, tax ID, payment instructions and brand color. All are optional; empty fields fall back to your workspace name and logo.
  3. Open a deal and go to the new Quote tab. The quote is addressed to the deal's company and contact.
  4. Add line items: a description, quantity and price for each. Subtotal and total update as you type.
  5. Set currency, discount, tax and validity. Choose how long the quote is valid: 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days, or no expiry.
  6. Write a message and terms for your client, or keep your defaults.
  7. Check the live preview. It's exactly what your client will see.
  8. Click Next: Review & share. The quote is saved and ready to send. Sending is covered in how to send a quote and get it accepted.

What goes on a quote

  • Your details: name, logo, address, email, phone, website and tax ID (for example a VAT or GST number).
  • Your client: the deal's company and contact, filled in from Twenty.
  • A quote number such as Q-0003, assigned in order when you send.
  • Line items with quantity, price and line total.
  • Subtotal, discount, tax and total in the deal's currency.
  • A valid-until date, a message, your terms, and payment instructions such as bank or UPI details.
  • Your brand color on the client page.

Tips

  • Set Stage for accepted deals to your pipeline's won stage. It defaults to CUSTOMER.
  • Save time with a default message and default terms. You can still change them on each quote.
  • Give quotes a short validity, such as 14 or 30 days, so clients decide sooner.
  • Discount and tax apply to the whole quote. For a discount on one item only, lower that line's price.

FAQ

Can I make a PDF quote from Twenty?

Yes. The client quote page prints to a tidy PDF from any browser, for you or your client.

Can everyone on my team create quotes?

Yes. QuoteFlowin is priced per Twenty workspace, not per user, and in the Quote tab each member can do only what Twenty already allows them to.

Does it work with self-hosted Twenty?

Yes, on Twenty 2.46 or later. Your server must be reachable from the internet so clients can open quote links. Email info@quoteflowin.com for install help.

Can I use different tax rates on different lines?

Not yet. Discount and tax apply to the whole quote.

Where are my quotes stored?

In your own Twenty workspace, next to your deals, under its permissions and backups.
