Refund Policy

When you can get your money back, and how to ask.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Try before you buy

Every workspace gets a free trial of 14 days or 10 sent quotes, with no card needed, so you can check QuoteFlowin fits before paying.

Monthly

Cancel at any time in the billing portal. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. We don't refund partial months.

Annual

If you're not happy, ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first annual payment. After that, you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid year, but we don't refund the remaining months. Renewals can be refunded within 14 days if you didn't use QuoteFlowin after renewing.

Lifetime

Ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, Lifetime purchases are not refundable, except if we shut QuoteFlowin down (below).

If we shut down QuoteFlowin

We will give at least 30 days' notice. Subscriptions will not renew. If you bought Lifetime within the 12 months before the shutdown date, we refund a pro-rated amount: $139 reduced by one twelfth for each full month since your purchase.

How to ask

Email info@quoteflowin.com from the address you used at checkout, with your Twenty workspace URL or invoice number. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Dodo Payments, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

Charged by mistake, or charged twice? Email us and we'll refund it in full. This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under consumer law.
