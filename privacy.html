Privacy Policy

What data QuoteFlowin handles, where it lives, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated 2 November 2026

In short

  • Your quotes and client details stay in your own Twenty workspace. We don't copy them anywhere.
  • We only receive what billing needs: your workspace ID and, when you buy a plan, your name and email.
  • We don't sell data, show ads or use tracking on our website.

Who we are

QuoteFlowin is run by Anudeep Kaur. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: info@quoteflowin.com.

Data in your Twenty workspace

QuoteFlowin stores quotes, line items and quote settings as records in your Twenty workspace, next to your deals. That includes client names and contact details you add, and, when a client opens a quote, the time it was first viewed and the name the client types to accept or decline.

This data is held by Twenty under your workspace, covered by your workspace's permissions and backups, and by Twenty's own privacy policy. You control it and can delete it at any time. For this data, you are the data controller and we do not receive a copy.

Data we receive

  • Workspace ID, to link your workspace to its plan.
  • Trial usage: when your trial started and how many quotes have been sent, so the trial limit works. No quote contents.
  • Name and email of the member who opens checkout, to create your customer record with Dodo Payments.
  • Purchase records from Dodo Payments: your plan, payment status and invoices. Never card numbers.
  • Emails you send us, to answer them.

We use this data only to run your trial and plan, handle billing and refunds, keep accounting records and support you.

Who we share it with

  • Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, which processes payments, tax and invoices.
  • Twenty, which hosts your workspace and runs QuoteFlowin.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

These providers may process data outside India. We don't sell or rent personal data.

This website

quoteflowin.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics and sets no cookies. GitHub receives your IP address to serve the pages, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

How long we keep it

We keep billing records as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 8 years in India. Other data we hold is deleted within 90 days of your plan ending, unless you ask sooner. Your quotes stay in your Twenty workspace until you delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing info@quoteflowin.com. We reply within 30 days. These rights apply under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, if you are in the EU or UK, under the GDPR. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

QuoteFlowin is meant for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post any update here with a new date, and email paying customers about important changes.
