How it works

Three steps from deal to won

No exports, no copy-paste, no second tool. Watch the whole flow, or jump to a step.

01

Create a quote from a deal

Open the deal's Quote tab. Add line items, discount, tax and a message. Totals update as you type.

02

Share a private link

Send the quote to get a link and a ready-written email. Send it from your own email app.

03

Client accepts, deal is won

Your client types their name and accepts. The deal moves to your won stage with the quote total as its amount.

Everything stays inside Twenty. Quotes live next to your deals.
